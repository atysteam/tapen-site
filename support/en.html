Support

Write to us with questions, problems or purchase requests. In the game, Settings > Contact Us opens a draft to the same address.

Refunds are requested through the store you downloaded the game from. App Store (iPhone and iPad): https://reportaproblem.apple.com Google Play (Android): https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637

You can get your permanent purchases back (Ad-Free Rewards, Unlimited Hints, costumes and packs) in the game with Settings > Purchases > Restore Purchases. You need to be signed in with the Apple Account or Google account you bought them with. Consumable items such as Hint packs and +2 tapes are not restored.
