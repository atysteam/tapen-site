You can earn skin parcels by completing levels. Their contents are random.

  • You can get a limited number of extra parcels by watching rewarded ads.
  • The odds are shown in the Game on the Parcel Odds screen, which you can open before a parcel opens.
  • Skins that can come from parcels are also sold singly. Winter Collection skins are only sold and never come from a parcel.
  • At set intervals a rarer skin is guaranteed; the current odds and guarantee rules are on the Parcel Odds screen.
  • If no eligible skin is left, you get Hints instead.
