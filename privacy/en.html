Tapen Privacy Policy

Version: 1.2
Effective date: 14 November 2026

Summary

  • Controller: The game's developer (named in section 1). Contact: support@tapengame.com
  • Game save: The game has no game server of its own and you don't need an account; it only reads a small settings file from our website when it opens (section 2.10). Your game save stays on your device. On iPhone and iPad, if iCloud is on, a copy is kept in your iCloud account with Apple; on Android, if your device's Google backup is on, the save may be included in that backup.
  • Personal data that reaches us: The emails you choose to send us; if you buy something on Android, Google Play's order record (section 2.6); if you joined a test version, your test record (section 2.11).
  • Ads: Ads in the game are shown by Google AdMob. We do not show you personalized ads and we do not track you across apps. Google collects its own data to show and measure ads (section 2.5).
  • Legal bases: Performance of a contract, legitimate interest and, where required, your consent. Each section names its basis.
  • Transfers: Data goes to Apple, Google, our website host and our email provider so they can provide these services. Their servers may be outside your country.
  • Your rights: Write to support@tapengame.com to use your rights under the GDPR or Turkish law (section 9).

This policy informs you under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). It covers the iPhone and iPad (iOS) and Android versions; parts that apply to one platform only say so.

1. Controller

Ahmet Tuna Yaşaroğlu, Türkiye. The game's developer; listed under this name as the seller on the App Store and under the developer name "Tapen" on Google Play.
Email: support@tapengame.com

2. What data, why, and on what legal basis

This data is obtained by automated means when you use the game, through the game itself and the Apple and Google software inside it; support emails are obtained when you send them to us.

2.1. Game save on your device

Data: Your progress, skins, Hint and ad counters, purchase entitlements, discoveries and settings.
Purpose: To run the game and let you continue where you left off.
This save is kept on your device and is not sent to us. If iCloud Backup or a computer backup is on, iOS may back it up together with other app data; Apple and you manage those backups. On Android, if your device's backup is on, Android may put this save, together with other app data, into the backup in your Google Account and restore it when you move to a new device; Google and you manage that backup. The save contains randomly generated device numbers. A separate copy of this device's number, kept on the device, is left out of the backup; after a restore on another device the game gives that device a new number.

2.2. iCloud save (iPhone and iPad)

The game keeps a copy of your save in Apple's key-value storage, in your own iCloud account. This brings your progress back on another device or after you delete and reinstall the game. The Android version has no iCloud or other cloud save.

  • Data: Completed levels and best results, game statistics, Hint records (including unused Hints), ad counters, parcels, owned and equipped skins and looks, cards seen and discoveries, the time of the save, Apple transaction numbers of applied purchases and randomly generated device numbers. No settings (language, sound) and no personal details.
  • Purpose: To carry and protect your progress across your devices.
  • Legal basis: Performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • Apple keeps this data under your Apple Account terms; we cannot see it. Purchase entitlements are always verified with the App Store, never from this copy.

2.3. Game Center (iPhone and iPad)

If you sign in to Game Center, the game sends Apple two things: the number of levels you have completed (leaderboard) and the achievements you earn.

  • Purpose: Leaderboard and achievements.
  • Legal basis: Performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • Apple shows and stores your nickname, profile and place on the leaderboard; other players may see them. The game does not store your Game Center identity. Signing in is optional; the game is fully playable without it. The Android version has no leaderboard or achievements and does not connect to Google Play Games.

2.4. Apple's and Google's crash reports and app analytics

The game contains no analytics or crash reporting tool of our own.

On iPhone and iPad, if "Share With App Developers" is on, Apple collects crash reports and usage information. Apple shares them with us in aggregate or in a form that does not identify you: downloads, sessions, country, device model, version, technical logs from a crash.

  • Purpose: To fix bugs and improve the game.
  • Legal basis: The permission Apple or Google asks you for, and our legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f), KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).
  • On iPhone and iPad, you can turn this off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

On Android, if sharing usage and diagnostics data with Google is on, Google Play collects crash and "app not responding" reports. Google shares them with us in Google Play Console in aggregate or in a form that does not identify you: device model, Android version, game version, technical logs from a crash. You can change this in your device's Google settings; the menu name depends on the device. Google Play also shows us aggregate statistics such as install and uninstall counts.

If you play a test version of the game through TestFlight, TestFlight also collects crash and usage information; this is Apple's data, and Apple passes it on to us.

2.5. Ads (Google AdMob)

Ads in the game are shown by Google AdMob.

We do not show you personalized ads. We don't ask for your advertising identifier: on iPhone and iPad we don't show iOS's tracking permission prompt, and on Android the game does not request the advertising ID permission.

Even for non-personalized ads, Google's ad software collects its own data to show and measure ads. According to Google's documentation this can include:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information, app-specific technical identifiers
  • Performance data such as launch time, and crash logs
  • The ads you see and your interactions with them
  • On Android also Google Play's app set ID and, where applicable, identifiers tied to accounts signed in on the device. Google Play's rules forbid using the app set ID for ad personalization or ad measurement. We do not read this identifier and do not combine it with any other identifier or personal data.

Purpose: To show ads, limit how often they appear, measure them and prevent fraud.

Legal basis: In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the consent needed to store and read information on your device is asked in the in-game consent window (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). Elsewhere, our legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f), KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).

For this data Google is an independent controller and applies its own policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your choices:

  • If you are in Europe, you can change your consent choice in the game's Settings screen.
  • The game has no forced ads; all ads are rewarded and optional, and you never have to watch them. With Ad-Free Rewards you get the rewards without watching ads, and no ads are shown to you.

2.6. Purchases

Apple processes purchases on iPhone and iPad, and Google Play on Android. We never see your card or payment details.

  • Data: Purchase entitlements verified on your device, and the stores' sales reports. Apple's reports are aggregate. Google Play gives us a record per order: order number, product, amount, date, device model and the buyer's country, state, city and postcode. We never see your name, email address or payment details.
  • Purpose: To deliver what you bought, restore permanent items and keep accounting records.
  • Legal basis: Performance of a contract and legal obligation (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (c), KVKK Art. 5(2)(c) and (ç)).

2.7. Support emails

The Contact Us button opens an editable draft in your email app. It is never sent automatically.

  • Data: Your email address, the name shown in your email account, what you write, and the details added to the draft: app version, operating system, device model, the game's language and the level you are on. You can delete these before sending.
  • Purpose: To answer you.
  • Legal basis: Handling your request and our legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f), KVKK Art. 5(2)(c) and (f)).
  • Service provider: Zoho Mail.
  • Retention: 2 years after the matter is closed, then deleted.

2.8. Consent records

The version of the Terms of Service you accepted at first launch, the version of the privacy notice presented to you, and your ad consent choice in Europe are stored on your device so you are not asked again.

2.9. Game engine

The game is made with Unity. Unity's analytics and diagnostics reporting is turned off in the game.

2.10. The game's settings file

When it opens, the game reads a small settings file from our website (tapengame.com). The file tells it, for example, whether the game needs an update. The game sends no information about you while doing this. As with any internet connection, however, the server that provides the file sees technical details of the request (connection details such as the IP address, the time and the browser/app type). The game also runs when this file cannot be read. The file can only switch something off (rewarded ads, the sale of a product) or say that an update is needed.

When you visit our website in a browser, the same kind of server logs are created. The site uses no cookies, analytics or ad tools.

  • Data: The connection's IP address, the time and the technical details of the request (server logs).
  • Purpose: Running the game with up-to-date settings and operating the website securely.
  • Legal basis: Providing the game and our legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f), KVKK Art. 5(2)(c) and (f)).
  • Service provider: GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages). https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
  • Retention: Kept under GitHub's own policies.

2.11. Test versions (TestFlight and Google Play closed testing)

If you use a test version of the game:

  • Data: The email address you gave us to add you to the test (kept in Apple's or Google's tester list) and the feedback you send: what you write, screenshots, device and version details.
  • Purpose: To run the test and fix bugs.
  • Legal basis: Handling your request and our legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f), KVKK Art. 5(2)(c) and (f)).
  • Retention: The tester list is deleted when the test ends. You can ask for earlier deletion.

3. What we don't collect

We don't collect:

  • Your name or email address (unless you write to us)
  • Card details, contacts, photos
  • Camera or microphone data
  • Precise location, health data
  • Your advertising identifier

The game does not ask for location, camera, microphone or tracking permission.

We don't sell your data. We don't make automated decisions that have legal effects on you.

4. Who receives data

Data may go only to these recipients, only for the purposes above:

  • Apple (iPhone and iPad): iCloud save, Game Center, purchases, crash reports and app analytics. https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google: ads; on Android also purchases (Google Play), device backup and crash reports (Google Play Console). https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • GitHub: website host that provides the game's settings file (GitHub Pages). https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
  • Zoho: support email. https://www.zoho.com/privacy.html
  • Authorities: where the law requires it.

We share your data only with these recipients, whose own terms and the applicable data protection rules require them to protect it at least as well as described in this policy.

5. International transfers

These providers' servers may be outside Turkey and the European Economic Area, for example in the United States. Because the controller is in Turkey, data from Europe may also be processed in Turkey. These providers describe the safeguards they use for international transfers (for example standard contractual clauses) in their own terms and policies. Write to us for details.

6. Age

Tapen is for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you are a parent and think your child has sent us data, write to us and we will delete it.

We show no one personalized ads. In Europe the age of digital consent is between 13 and 16 depending on the country. If you are below it, talk to a parent before giving consent.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Save on your device: Deleted when you delete the game.
  • Copy in iCloud (iPhone and iPad): Deleting the game does not delete it; your progress comes back when you reinstall. To delete it: in the game, Settings > Delete cloud save. This deletes the iCloud copy and the save on this device together; it cannot be undone. Unused Hints are lost too. If another device with iCloud on uses the same Apple Account, its save may be uploaded again; to delete everywhere, delete on those devices too or first turn off iCloud for this app.
  • Android device backup: Deleting the game does not delete it right away; the backup is kept under your Google Account's rules. You can manage or turn off backup in your device's Settings; the menu name depends on the device.
  • Support emails: Kept for 2 years after the matter is closed, then deleted. You can ask for earlier deletion.
  • Sales records: Kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Server logs: Logs created when the settings file is read are kept at GitHub under GitHub's own policies.
  • Data held by Apple and Google: Kept under their own policies.

Because the game has no account, we don't hold the data on your device. We can't view or delete it for you; you can delete it yourself by deleting the game.

8. Withdrawing consent

  • Ad consent in Europe: In the game's Settings screen.
  • Apple's analytics sharing (iPhone and iPad): Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.
  • Google's usage and diagnostics sharing (Android): In your device's Google settings.
  • Android backup: In the backup section of your device's Settings; if you turn it off, the save stays only on the device.
  • iCloud save: Turn it off for this app in the iCloud section of your Apple Account in iPhone Settings; the save then stays only on the device.
  • Game Center: You can sign out of Game Center; the game plays without it.

Withdrawing consent doesn't stop you from playing and doesn't affect what happened before.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection.

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you may:

  • Learn whether your data is processed and request information about it
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether data is used for it
  • Know the third parties it is transferred to
  • Request correction, deletion or destruction, and that third parties be notified of this
  • Object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis
  • Claim compensation for damage

To use your rights, write to support@tapengame.com. We answer free of charge within 30 days. We may ask you for additional information to confirm that the request comes from you.

You can also complain:

  • Your national data protection authority: https://www.edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en
  • In Turkey: the Personal Data Protection Board, https://www.kvkk.gov.tr

10. Security

We keep the data we receive to a minimum, store nothing on servers of our own, and limit access to our provider accounts. The game's internet connections (settings file, ads, purchases) are encrypted. No method gives absolute security.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. For important changes we show the new text in the game. The version and effective date are at the top.

12. Contact

Email: support@tapengame.com
The controller's identity is given in section 1.
